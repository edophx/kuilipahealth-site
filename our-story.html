Our founders’ story

On October 3, 2021, Edo and Devin met in Phoenix and felt at home with each other from the start. Edo had been down the aisle once before and wasn’t planning a return trip. Yet the man who said “never” was the one who asked, “Will you marry me?” On October 3, 2024, three years to the day after they met, they said “I do.”

Blending a family together taught them what it means to be accepted exactly as you are. Now they’re building Kui Lipa Health side by side, to pass that welcome on to every patient and every kind of family. Devin leads the care. He spent years in patient navigation and will complete his family nurse practitioner degree in 2027. Edo leads the business as executive director, bringing more than 20 years of healthcare technology experience so every call and visit runs smoothly.

The name holds their story too: kui, the mesquite of Devin’s Tohono Oʼodham homeland, and lipa, the linden of Edo’s Bosnia.

Our name

Two trees from our founders’ homelands, rooted side by side.

Kui is Tohono Oʼodham for the mesquite tree.

Lipa is Bosnian for the linden tree.
