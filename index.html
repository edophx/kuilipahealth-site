Our name

Kui is Tohono Oʼodham for the mesquite tree.
Lipa is Bosnian for the linden tree.
Two trees from our founders’ homelands, standing side by side.

Our mission

We provide personal family care so every patient feels heard and respected.

Our vision

Like the shade of the mesquite and the linden, we offer comfort when life feels heavy, safety to be yourself, and a welcome for whoever you call family.

What is a family nurse practitioner?

A family nurse practitioner (FNP) is an advanced practice nurse trained to care for patients of all ages. In Arizona, nurse practitioners can examine and diagnose patients, order tests and prescribe medications without physician supervision.
